About

A bit about how I work

I've spent the last ten years working in mental health and healthcare, starting in the UK's NHS and major charities before retraining as a counsellor at Adler University. I have a passion for being with people through the toughest moments in life. When others look away, I sit shoulder to shoulder with my clients, and we slow things down together.

I approach therapy from an emotion-focused, trauma-informed, somatic angle. I want clients to leave each session feeling something: less afraid of their emotional responses, and more attuned to the needs underneath them. I also offer EMDR to support trauma reprocessing.

Speaking

Talks & workshops

I love getting out and meeting people. I've spoken at wellness brunches, corporate events and charity fundraisers on topics like learning to listen to the cues your body sends you, and reclaiming self-care as something you live day to day rather than earn or buy. I host workshops designed so people leave feeling something in their body and carrying a practical tool they can use.

For organizers: speaker bio

Rachael Cruickshanks is a pre-registered counsellor who is passionate about helping people understand themselves better and build lives that actually feel like their own. Her background spans mental health, digital healthcare, charities and palliative care. Rachael brings a practical, evidence-informed perspective to conversations about relationships, boundaries and the patterns that shape our lives. She believes we often have more insight and strength than we give ourselves credit for, and that the first step is simply learning to listen to ourselves.
